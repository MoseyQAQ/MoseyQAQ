## Research Interests
* I am interested in __computational condensed matter physics__.
* In particular, I focus on __ferroelectric materials__, especially their dynamic response under an external field (e.g., Switching, Piezoelectricity, Thermal Conducivity).
* To understand them, I often adopt __a multi-scale approach__: Density Functional Theory + Molecular Dynamics + Machine Learning Force Fields + Phenomenological Theory.
* Besides, I am also interested in the development of new machine learning force fields.
